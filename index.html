Data sources and formulas

- HP (est.) = body-part (BodyPartDef) HP sum x the race baseHealthScale. Body-part HP follows Data/Core/Defs/Bodies and each mod's definitions.
- Armor shows the race raw ArmorRating_Blunt / Sharp / Heat values. B = blunt, S = sharp, H = heat.
- Melee DPS = the highest power / cooldownTime across all attack tools.
- Ranged DPS = burst x damage / (warmup + (burst-1) x ticksBetweenBurstShots / 60 + cooldown). Beam weapons use the Beam damage definition base damage.
- Theoretical values: accuracy, misses, armor mitigation, shields, and explosion radius are not factored into DPS. HP may differ from the in-game display.
- Profile images and company logos load from the IMAGES/Profile and IMAGES/Logo folders. Support units have no profile image.
