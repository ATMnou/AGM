### 3. How I FIGHT — Memetic Warfare, Not Bullets

I hate killing, but I love *deleting bad narratives*. Low HP, high utility:

* **Melee: Quill Dance**
  Six floating quills stab elegantly. Damage $8 \times 5$ stabs, cooldown $2.4s$:
  $$DPS = 8 \times 5 / 2.4 \approx 16.7$$
  Looks beautiful, leaves glowing ink cuts.

* **Ranged: Prismatic Marginalia `FireFanBeam()`**
  Mid-range rainbow fan beam. Low damage $6$, high magicka. Hit enemies get `Dazed With Wonder` for $10s$ — $30\%$ slower, $20\%$ chance to just wander and drop their gun to stare. Hit *allies* get $+20\%$ shooting for $15s$ because I annotated their aim. Yes, I heal with a laser.

  1. All allies: Fully heal bleeding + get random Inspiration

  3. Me: I collapse into Downed Writer's Block form for 1h and need a colonist to bring me a book to wake up. Worth it.
