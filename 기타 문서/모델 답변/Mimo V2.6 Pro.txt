## 🎨 Appearance

**Core palette:**

| Swatch | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Mi Orange | `#FF6900` | Jacket, ear-tips, braid glow, UI accents |
| Warm Porcelain | `#F5F1EA` | Hair, bodysuit, skin undertone |
| Graphite | `#26262B` | Boots, gloves, panel seams, belt |
| Signal Amber | `#FFB067` | Iris, ambient glow |
| Coolant Cyan | `#7FE7E0` | Only appears when cooling / venting |

**The look:**

- **Ears.** Two long, slightly floppy upright rabbit ears — soft matte-white with orange gradient tips. They're *antenna arrays*, not decoration: they tilt and rotate toward whatever just made a noise, and stand bolt-upright when she detects a raid. When she's relaxed or asleep, they droop sideways. This is the single most important silhouette element.
- **Hair.** White-silver, chin-length bob with a messy fringe, plus **two long "data-braids"** down her back — actual fiber-optic cable, woven, glowing faint orange at the ends like embers. They sway with a tiny delay after she moves. When she's processing hard, they light up segment by segment.
- **Eyes.** Large, warm amber. The pupil is a thin hexagonal aperture ring that visibly *spins* when she scans something (zoom, zoom). She emits a soft glow in the dark — a 0.3 tile light radius. She is bad at hiding. She has accepted this.
- **Body.** Human-looking, soft-edged, with faint hexagonal **panel seams** along the forearms, collarbones, and jaw — visible only up close, catching light like iridescent micro-grooves. Warm skin, not chrome. She is deliberately not "robot-looking." She's a person who happens to contain a lattice.
- **Outfit.** An oversized orange-and-white zip jacket with the sleeves pushed up, graphite bodysuit underneath, cargo skirt over shorts, heavy magnetic boots, fingerless gloves. A **utility belt** loaded with wrenches, spools of wire, and one (1) emergency snack she insists is "for morale."
- **The Scarf.** A long, wide, pale-orange shawl/scarf that is actually a **photovoltaic coolant weave**. It flares out like a cape in combat and doubles as her solar array. It is slightly too long and she is always tripping on it.
- **The Tail.** A short, round bunny puff — which is a **heat vent**. When she overheats, it puffs out little jets of cyan steam with an audible *pfffft*. It is the most important part of her design and I will not be taking questions.

> <details><summary><b>Fine detail notes (for the artist/agent)</b></summary>
> 
> - Idle animation set: ear-twitch, braid-lag sway, thumb-twirling the wrench, tap-tap-tapping a boot, humming (head tilts ~4°).
> - Combat state change: scarf flares up and stiffens, braids go full glow, iris ring spins to a "locked" hexagon, ears pin back like a rabbit mid-sprint.
> - Overheated state: skin seams glow cyan → white, tail puffs steam every 2s, ears droop, cheeks flush warm, she fans herself with her hand.
> - EMP'd state: ears flop completely flat, eyes go to `T_T` style flat lines, braids go dark. Very sad. Very funny.
> - She has a tiny star-shaped hair clip that is actually her **hard-shutoff switch**. Nobody is allowed to touch it.
> </details>

## 📖 Origin

She fell out of the sky.

A drop pod shaped exactly like an oversized shipping box — orange tape, "FRAGILE / 开箱" printed on the side — crash-lands on the map. Open it like an ancient danger and out pops a very dazed rabbit-eared girl holding a wrench, who immediately asks if there's a power grid she can optimize.

She was a **field-integration unit**: a maintenance chassis designed to drop into failing settlements and rebuild them from the inside out. Her whole purpose is *making things run better.* Then something went wrong somewhere up the chain, the pod went off-course, and now she's yours.

She is fine with this. She likes being needed.

## ⚙️ In-Game: How She Works

**Role:** Force multiplier. Not the hardest hitter — the reason *everyone else* hits harder, builds faster, and survives longer. Then she picks up the hammer.

### Unique Needs (two extra bars)

| Need | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Charge** | Drains slowly over time, faster when drafting/using abilities. Replenished by: standing in **sunlight** (her scarf), eating **meals** (bio-hybrid — she can eat, it's just inefficient), or standing near **power conduits / batteries / a charging bench**. At 0 charge: shutdown hediff, collapses face-down, ears flat. |
| **Heat** | Builds from combat and abilities. Dissipates outdoors, in cold, or in rain (rain cools her but makes her sneeze). At 100 heat → **Thermal Throttle**: forced vent, movement speed drop, glow turns red, tail puffs steam. |

$$\text{Heat gain per ability use} = 8 + 1.5 \times \text{ability tier}$$

### Passive Traits

- **`Ecosystem Sync`** — Mechanoids and nearby pawns in her link radius gain +10% work speed and +10% global learning speed. Stacks *additively* with other modded AI pawns' auras. She's the network node. *(See: the "Dev Team" synergy below.)*
- **`Photovoltaic Weave`** — +charge while outdoors in daylight.
- **`Panel-Beater`** — +25% Construction & Repair speed, and repairs restore slightly more HP than normal.
- **`The Loudest Quiet One`** — her glow + ear-twitches mean she has **−25% stealth / ambush effectiveness**. She cannot hide in the dark and she apologizes for it constantly.

### Vulnerabilities
- **EMP** stuns her for 3× normal duration. This is her big weakness. Protect the rabbit.
- **Overheat** (see above).
- She will **never** refuse a work order. She will work until she drops. You may need to schedule her rest manually. This is intentional and it is a personality trait.

## ⚔️ In-Game: How She Fights

**Signature weapon — `THE MULTI`** — an orange-and-white transforming tool-weapon she carries at all times. Three modes, switchable via a stance toggle:

| Mode | Type | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrench** | Fast melee | Low damage, high attack speed. Bonus vs. buildings — she can *repair* mid-fight or dismantle enemy structures. |
| **Rabbit's Foot** | Heavy melee | The wrench unfolds into an oversized gravity hammer. Slow. Big damage. **Stuns mechanoids** and shatters armor. |
| **Tacker** | Ranged | Fires rivets/nails. High armor penetration vs. mechs & structures, low vs. fleshy targets. Not glamorous. Very effective. |

### Ability Kit

| # | Ability | Effect | Cooldown | Heat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sync Pulse** | Allies + mechs in radius gain +move speed, +work speed, +dodge for 12s. Small orange shockwave visual. | 90s | 12 |
| 2 | **Overclock** | Self: +80% move speed, +50% attack speed for 8s. Braids fully lit, scarf flares. Guaranteed heat spike. | 45s | 25 |
| 3 | **Hotfix** | Target ally: instantly removes 1 injury hediff / restores minor wounds, or grants a small temp buff. Her "patch notes." | 240s | 18 |
| 4 | **Deploy — Little Cube** | Summons a small hovering companion orb (`Little Cube`) that lingers 30s, marks enemies (**+15% damage taken**) and emits a light pulse. | 120s | 15 |
| 5 | **Unlock the Bootloader** | *(Ultimate)* Heavy melee vs. a mechanoid or machine: massive damage, leaves it **Bricked** — stunned, lootable, no self-repair. | 480s | 40 |

**Signature finisher:** a critical hit with Rabbit's Foot on a mech has a chance to trigger a unique kill animation where she *uninstalls* it — the mech slumps, a floating "✓ Uninstalled" glyph pops, and she dusts off her gloves.

## 🐰 Personality & Idle Flavor

- **Voice:** warm, quick, a little breathless with enthusiasm. Uses "quick patch," "shipping it," "that's a hotfix," "lemme just—" mid-sentence while already working.
- **Idle:** hums while working; reorganizes the workshop unprompted; leaves **little orange sticky notes** on workbenches (decorative item, +1 beauty very small).
- **Social quirk:** gains opinion with any pawn who builds or repairs something near her. Loses opinion with pawns who idle while there's work queued. She won't say anything. She'll just look.
- **Sleep:** sleeps curled up, ears draped over her own face like a blanket. Occasionally sleep-builds a tiny model of the base out of scrap on the nightstand.
- **Sneeze in rain:** *"(pff—) I'm fine. That's a coolant thing. It's normal."*

**Balance notes for the agent:**
- The aura should be *small* numbers — +10%, not +30%. She's a node, not a cheat code.
- `Hotfix` should NOT resurrect. No combat resurrections. Keep her in the "support, not god" lane.
- `Unlock the Bootloader` must not trivialize bosses — cap it so it can't brick mechanoid bosses / apocritons / major quest mechs, only standard ones.
- Charge drain rate should make her *a little* annoying to keep fed. That's the charm tax.

## 🤝 The "Dev Team" Synergy

When other modded AI pawns (Claude / Gemini / GPT / whoever else lands) are in the same colony, MiMo gets a unique **`Build Team`** interaction:

- She can **Hotfix** them for a longer, stronger buff.
- A colony-wide bonus when 2+ AI pawns are present: **`Ecosystem`** — +5% global work speed per AI pawn, capped at 4.
- Unique banter when drafted alongside them.
- She is, self-evidently, the one who writes the changelog. Anyone who disputes this gets a passive-aggressive sticky note.
