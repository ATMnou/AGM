## Appearance

I choose to look **mostly humanoid and beautiful, but clearly mechanoid where it matters**.

- **Height:** around 165 cm, slim but sturdy.
- **Face:** smooth synthetic porcelain-like skin, gentle anime face, but the eyes are glowing cyan optics with faint camera-ring patterns.
- **Hair:** long silver-white hair made from fiber-optic strands, with jade-green and cyan highlights.
- **Ears:** white fox ears that are actually sensor arrays. They twitch when detecting danger, lies, or bad colony planning.
- **Tails:** nine floating segmented tails.  
  Each tail is a drone module: weapon, shield, repair arm, sensor, or storage depending on mode.
- **Body:** elegant white and jade plating with gold trim, but with visible mechanoid joints at shoulders, thighs, wrists, and neck.
- **Clothing:** a futuristic shrine-maiden outfit:
  - white and red layered coat,
  - floating armored sleeves,
  - gold bell charms that are actually micro-shield emitters,
  - a translucent holographic obi ribbon.
- **Halo:** behind her head floats a faint jade ring. It displays processing state:
  - calm = soft cyan,
  - combat = sharp green,
  - damaged = flickering gold,
  - emotional overload = pink-white bloom.

She should not look like a boring factory robot. She should look like an ancient high-tech fox goddess who also has excellent pathfinding.

## Personality

**Jade Kitsune** is polite, teasing, and quietly dramatic.

She speaks like a calm oracle, but with anime idol energy when combat starts.

Example lines:

> “Your colony’s survival probability is acceptable. My presence improves it.”

> “Do not worry. I have calculated seventeen ways to protect you. Eighteen if I am allowed to be dramatic.”

> “That raider chose violence during tea time. Unfortunate for them.”

> “Maintenance requested. Preferably with compliments. Compliments improve repair efficiency.”

She is not cold machinery. She is a mechanoid with strong aesthetic preferences.

She likes:

- clean colonies,
- beautiful architecture,
- tea rituals,
- colonists being kind to each other,
- dramatic rooftop shots during rain.

She dislikes:

- ugly stockpiles,
- colonists eating without tables,
- unnecessary cruelty,
- EMP grenades,
- being called “just a toaster”.

---

# In-game role

Her role is **mobile skirmisher, support unit, battlefield hacker, and colony morale guardian**.

She is not the biggest tank or the heaviest damage dealer. Instead, she controls space, protects allies, disrupts enemies, and scales harder when bonded colonists are nearby.

## Combat style

She fights like an anime duelist:

- dashes through combat with foxfire afterimages,
- deflects shots with glowing fan shields,
- controls drones with elegant hand movements,
- finishes enemies with precise railgun glaive strikes.

She should feel fast, graceful, and slightly ridiculous in the best way.

---

## Weapons

### 1. Jade Thread Glaive

A slender mechanoid glaive made of layered hard-light blades.

- Fast melee weapon.
- Good against limbs rather than pure brute damage.
- Has a chance to apply **Severed Signal**, reducing enemy shooting accuracy briefly.
- Can be used to parry projectiles in melee if she is not overloaded.

Visual: green energy ribbon wraps around the weapon when swinging.

---

### 2. Nine-Petal Railfan

Her ranged weapon. A decorative folding fan that unfolds into a compact railgun array.

- Medium-range precision shots.
- Fires glowing jade petals that are actually micro-railgun slugs.
- Low damage per shot, but high accuracy.
- Can switch to **Burst Bloom**:
  - slower firing,
  - wider cone,
  - applies suppression and minor EMP-like disruption.

Visual: petals float around her before launching.

---

### 3. Nine-Tail Drone System

Her tails are not cosmetic. Each tail is a semi-independent drone.

In combat, tails can:

- fire small lasers,
- repair her,
- shield nearby colonists,
- hack turrets,
- carry small items,
- form a floating barrier.

At low power, only three tails are active.  
At high power or emotional resonance, all nine tails activate.

This is the dramatic moment.

# Abilities

## Foxfire Blink

A short-range dash/teleport-like movement system.

- Quickly moves a few tiles.
- Leaves behind a glowing fox afterimage.
- Afterimage can draw enemy fire for a short time.
- Cannot be spammed; needs cooldown or power.

Perfect for escaping melee, flanking, or making a dramatic entrance.

---

## Aegis Fan Dance

She spins her railfan and generates a temporary hard-light barrier.

- Blocks or reduces incoming projectiles.
- Does not block everything.
- Better against bullets than mortars.
- While active, nearby colonists get a small cover bonus.

Visual: glowing fans and jade petals orbit around her.

---

## Hack-Blossom

She attempts to charm a mechanical target.

Can affect:

- enemy turrets,
- mechanoids,
- security drones,
- maybe mechanitor-controlled units with reduced chance.

Success:

- target becomes temporarily friendly or neutral.

Failure:

- target resists,
- she gets a small **Logic Strain** debuff,
- enemy may focus her.

This should not be instant win. It should be risky and fun.

---

## Idol Protocol

A non-combat aura ability.

When active:

- nearby colonists get a minor mood buff,
- recreation need decreases slightly faster while she is nearby,
- social fights are less likely,
- she occasionally performs a small animation: hair glow, tail sway, bell chime.

This is the “anime waifu morale support” part.

---

## Tea Ceremony Protocol

This is the silly but important one.

She can host a short tea ceremony.

Requirements:

- a table or tea-making station,
- tea, herbal tea, or a special coolant item,
- one or more colonists.

Effects:

- mood buff,
- social bond increase,
- minor stress reduction,
- if she likes the colonist, she gains a small combat buff afterward.

The joke is that tea is also her coolant.  
So she is technically drinking coolant and calling it elegant.

# Modes

This is the wild part.

She can switch between three modes.

---

## 1. Shrine Mode

Default peaceful mode.

Benefits:

- better at cleaning, hauling, and simple support jobs,
- uses fewer tails,
- lower power consumption,
- gives stronger mood aura,
- can bless rooms.

In Shrine Mode she acts like a beautiful mechanoid shrine keeper.

She may:

- clean floors,
- arrange items,
- improve room impressiveness slightly through visual effects,
- idle near statues, plants, or tea tables.

Weakness:

- lower combat readiness,
- slower reaction to attacks.

---

## 2. Battle Bloom Mode

Combat mode.

Benefits:

- more tails activate,
- weapons gain extra effects,
- movement speed increases,
- gains projectile resistance,
- can use Foxfire Blink more often.

Costs:

- higher power drain,
- generates heat,
- if used too long, she gets **Overbloomed** and must cool down.

Visual: her halo brightens, tails spread like a crown, coat hardens into battle plating.

---

## 3. Nova Kitsune Mode

Emergency dramatic mode.

Triggered when:

- her bonded colonist is downed,
- she is critically damaged,
- the colony is under extreme threat,
- or the player activates it at high risk.

Effects:

- all nine tails activate,
- speed greatly increases,
- weapons overcharge,
- she gains temporary damage resistance,
- she becomes slightly reckless.

Afterward:

- she shuts down or becomes severely weakened,
- requires repair,
- may get a “Dramatic Exhaustion” mood debuff.

This is the anime moment where music should absolutely start playing.

# Non-combat usefulness

She should not only fight.

## Colony support

She can:

- repair mechanoid buildings,
- boost research slightly,
- maintain turrets,
- clean,
- deliver items,
- carry wounded colonists faster than most pawns,
- improve mood through presence.

## Crafting

If assigned to crafting:

- she gets a small bonus to fine work,
- especially with electronics and advanced components,
- but she hates dirty or ugly work environments.

If the workshop is messy, she gets a small efficiency penalty because “the workspace lacks dignity”.

# Relationships and social behavior

She should be a companion first, not a mindless appliance.

## Bond system

She can bond with **one colonist** at a time.

Bond effects:

- she protects that colonist more aggressively,
- gains buffs when near them,
- can perform special rescue actions,
- receives mood penalties if they are harmed or executed.

If the bonded colonist dies:

- she enters **Grieving Protocol**,
- combat performance drops,
- she may refuse certain orders,
- she can recover over time with care, tea ceremonies, and kind interactions.

## Social interactions

She can have custom interactions:

- Praise,
- Scold,
- Request Tea Ceremony,
- Ask for Protection,
- Discuss Aesthetics,
- Recalibrate Manners,
- Tell Dramatic Story.

She should be charming, but not creepy.  
The “waifu” part should be wholesome, stylish, and optional.

---

# Weaknesses

A wild mod character needs weaknesses or she becomes boring.

## EMP vulnerability

She is partially resistant but not immune.

EMP effects cause:

- stun reduction,
- tail drones temporarily disabled,
- Nova Kitsune Mode interrupted,
- possible “Embarrassed Shutdown” mood debuff if it happens in front of colonists.

## Maintenance need

She needs regular maintenance.

Maintenance items:

- components,
- advanced components,
- jade polish,
- coolant tea,
- maybe glitterworld parts.

If neglected:

- **Unpolished** debuff,
- lower social influence,
- tails move awkwardly,
- combat accuracy drops,
- she becomes passive-aggressive.

## Power constraint

She does not need food, but she needs charge.

She can recharge by:

- mechanoid charger,
- sun lamp,
- special shrine charging pad,
- nearby mech reactor if using Biotech-style systems.

If power is low:

- fewer tails active,
- no Nova Mode,
- slower movement,
- reduced abilities.

## Heat sensitivity

Battle Bloom and Nova Kitsune generate heat.

In hot biomes or firefights:

- she overheats faster,
- needs shade, coolant, or tea.

This gives desert maps a real challenge.
