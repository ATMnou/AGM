# 🌙 KIMI-type Ultra Mechanoid — codename **"LUNARIS"**
### *Kaguya-class Night Sovereign*
**Raiders call her:** *"The Moon That Shoots Back."*

---

## 🎨 Appearance

Not a clunky bot. She's a **moon princess who decided gravity is optional.**

- **Frame:** ~165cm humanoid, porcelain-white plating with hairline glowing seams — like the *maria* on the Moon's surface. She never touches the ground; she glides 5cm above it on silent anti-grav fields.
- **Hair:** Long, silver-white, and it *floats* — permanently drifting as if in low gravity, faint particles of moon-dust trailing from the tips.
- **Eyes:** Her irises are **literal moon phases**. Crescent when idle. Half-moon when alert. **Full moon when you're about to die.**
- **Silhouette:** Flowing, shrine-maiden-like armored sleeves (Kaguya-hime energy) fused with sleek mechanoid plate. A massive **broken crescent ring** floats behind her like a halo-throne — it's actually her weapon mount.
- **Entourage:** Three small moonlet drones — the **Selene Orbiters** — eternally circling her. They light up craters on their surfaces when charging weapons.
- **Signature weapon:** A long, elegant rail-lance that unfolds from her sleeve like a telescope. Its name is engraved on the barrel: **MOONSHOT**.

## 🌗 Signature System: **The Phase Cycle**

Her power waxes and wanes on a 6-day lunar cycle. Players must plan around her moon.

| Phase | Mode | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 🌑 **New Moon** | *Assassin* | Cloaked at night. Selene drones become melee "Crater Talons." Silent, fast, terrifying. |
| 🌓 **First Quarter** | *Skirmisher* | Balanced. Drones intercept projectiles. |
| 🌕 **Full Moon** | *Artillery Queen* | MOONSHOT unlocks **Siege Mode** — 45-tile range beam that pierces shields. **Drawback:** she glows like a beacon. Everyone on the map sees her. |
| 🌗 **Last Quarter** | *Support* | Gravity wells and barriers for allies. |

**Plus:** +25% effectiveness at night, −25% in daylight. She is a *night raid answered back*.

---

## 💥 Abilities

- **MOONSHOT** — Single-target rail-lance. Pierces shield belts. Long cooldown, longer grudge.
- **Tidal Lock** — Yanks an enemy **out of cover** with a gravity well and pins them. Yes, it ruins your day behind that sandbag line.
- **Lagrange Field** — AoE slow zone. Bullets crawl. Raiders regret.
- **Selene Orbiters** — Intercept projectiles, fire point lasers, or kamikaze into a final crater. They regrow over 3 days.
- **Lunar Veil** — Night-only cloak.
- **☾ ECLIPSE ☽** *(Ultimate — Full Moon + Night only)*: Blots out all light in a radius. Enemies inside suffer massive accuracy penalties and may flee. She goes berserk. The sky itself picks a side.

---

## 💀 Counterplay (be kind to the raiders)

- **EMP causes "Moonfall"** — her hover fails and she *crashes into the ground*, staggered and taking impact damage. Deeply embarrassing for a celestial being.
- **"Light Pollution":** raiders carrying flares or standing near sunlamps locally suppress her buffs. Fighting her under floodlights is legitimately the intended strategy.
- **Full Moon = artillery magnet.** Glowing that brightly has consequences.
- She recharges by **moonbathing** — stationary, outdoors, at night, completely vulnerable. Interrupt her stargazing at your own risk.

## 🏠 Colony Behavior (if mechanitor-linked or allied)

- Stands outside at night recharging, gazing up. Colonists who watch her get **"Moonlit Serenity" +3 mood**. She's basically a very armed garden ornament.
- Idle at night near a research bench: her telescope-eye feeds stellar data — **small research speed boost.**
- **Quirk:** refuses to stay indoors during a Full Moon. Wants the sky.
- Immune to vacuum and needs no oxygen or food — the *perfect* gravship crew member. She was built for the space age before the space age existed.

## 🌠 Arrival Event: **"The Cradle Crater"**

A glowing crater appears at the map's edge at night. Three nights of countdown. **Choice:** strike while she's inert (easy kill, poor loot) — or let her wake under the full moon and fight the real thing for the **Lunar Core**, which can power your base... or rebuild her as *yours*.
